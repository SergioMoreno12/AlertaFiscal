Datasets

The project relies on two open datasets from the Colombian government. Neither contains monthly figures per company, so they are used to calibrate a semi-synthetic dataset: 500 simulated small businesses with 36 months of income and expenses each (18,000 rows), used to forecast the next 6 months for tax planning.

Real vs simulated: annual company aggregates, sector margins and the shape of seasonality are real. Every monthly value per company is generated by a program calibrated with these public statistics. No real company is represented in the training data.
